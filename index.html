What this is (and isn't)

Everything in this lab is an independent project, built on my own time using general, public principles. None of it is affiliated with, endorsed by, or built using confidential material from any employer, past or present. Figures and scenarios in these tools are indicative estimates to aid discussion — not a substitute for formal risk assessment or legal advice.
